# Awesome-Corporate-Card-Platform

## Top Corporate Card Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Corporate Cards, Spend Management, Expense Controls, Virtual Cards, Real-Time Budgets & Automated Reconciliation*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Corporate Cards** and modern spend management. These tools issue physical and virtual cards, enforce real-time budgets and controls, automate expense capture and reconciliation, and give finance teams visibility and control over company spending.



**Examples** include Ramp, Brex, Rho, Airbase, Mesh Payments, Pleo, Moss, Divvy by BILL, Emburse Cards, and Soldo (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: True open-source corporate card issuing is rare due to banking and regulatory requirements. This section is expanded with the strongest available open-source expense trackers, spend management tools, and self-hosted finance systems that can complement or partially replace commercial card platforms for tracking, budgeting, and reporting.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Firefly III](https://github.com/firefly-iii/firefly-iii)**  

  Mature, self-hosted personal and small-business finance manager with double-entry bookkeeping, budgets, rules, multi-currency support, and strong expense tracking.



- **[Wally](https://github.com/polius/Wally)**  

  Lightweight, self-hosted expense tracker with clean UI, categorization, tagging, and CSV import/export capabilities.



- **[Impensa](https://github.com/neiios/impensa)**  

  Simple open-source expense management application with visualization, categorization, and export features.



- **[Splitpro / open expense-sharing tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source alternatives to Splitwise for group and team expense splitting, useful for internal cost allocation.



- **[Steedos Expense / open expense control systems](https://github.com/steedos)**  

  Open-source expense and cost-control applications built on low-code platforms, supporting budgets, approvals, and reporting.



- **[Bank-statement parsers and spend analyzers](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects that ingest credit-card or bank statements, categorize transactions, and produce spending insights.



- **[Custom virtual-card and spend-limit prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental open-source tools exploring card-like controls, budgets, and approval workflows (not production issuing systems).



- **[Accounting and bookkeeping open-source cores](https://github.com/)**  

  Systems such as GnuCash, Ledger, or hledger that can serve as the backend for custom spend tracking.



- **[Receipt OCR and expense capture libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source components for extracting data from receipts and feeding expense management workflows.



- **[Self-hosted finance dashboards](https://github.com/)**  

  Projects that combine transaction data, budgets, and visualizations for internal spend visibility.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Actual Budget, Maybe Finance, and other self-hosted budgeting applications.

- ERPNext / Odoo expense and accounting modules for more complete business finance needs.

- Open-source AP and invoice tools that can sit alongside card data.

- Rule engines and categorization ML models for automatic expense classification.

- Privacy-focused personal finance trackers adapted for small-team use.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Commercial corporate cards handle issuance, underwriting, and payment networks. For the tracking and control layer, combine statement imports or bank feeds with **Firefly III** or similar self-hosted tools, add approval workflows via open low-code platforms, and surface budgets and reports in custom dashboards. True card issuing remains regulated and is best left to licensed providers; open-source shines in visibility, categorization, and internal controls.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Corporate cards involve regulated financial services (issuing, credit, payments). Open-source tools can manage expenses and reporting but **cannot** replace licensed card issuers or banking partners.

- Always ensure compliance with accounting standards, tax rules, and internal controls when handling company spend data. Self-hosted systems require strong security and backup practices.
